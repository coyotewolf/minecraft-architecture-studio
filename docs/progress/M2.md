## Implemented

The React page now renders a canonical, deterministic 288-cell two-floor house, plus an empty project. It has real Three.js geometry, six camera presets, reset/focus, orbit/pan/zoom, keyboard camera and center-reticle selection, touch navigation, a full block inspector and responsive layout. Geometry covers the fixture's cube/log/stair/slab/door/bed/glass cases; each mesh part maps directly to the canonical UID. Inspector reports actual XYZ, full normalized state, category, evidence and edit status.

Pure geometry handles all 40 stair facing/half/shape combinations and door facing/open/hinge/half states. Unsupported geometry is displayed as a wireframe with an explicit warning. Schematic colors are deliberate; no Minecraft textures or raw model assets are distributed. Bed mattress/legs are analytic cuboids, not a complete textured entity renderer.

Canonical core contracts, registry and engine are unchanged. Coordinates are rebased only for rendering, selection never floors a boundary hit, and camera gestures do not mutate design data. All fixture records are explicitly hand-authored user_created data. No photo analysis, AI output or fake inventory totals are shown.
